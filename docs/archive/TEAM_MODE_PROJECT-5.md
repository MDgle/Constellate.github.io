## Goal
A new option on the New game page: Teams (the Classic / Custom tabs were removed in step 3b; the page is what Custom used to be). When on, each seat gets a team selector (team 1, 2 or 3). Scoring stays individual; the winner is decided by team.

## Rules (all decided by the owner; do not re-litigate, log changes at the bottom)
1. Setup: New game page (and the in-room new game page). 4 players only, not Solo. At least 2 distinct teams (all on one team is blocked; all separate is just a normal game). 3v1 is allowed.
2. Scoring: individual per sector, exactly as now. Turn order, the Fleet pool (rank) and Deficit Elimination all use INDIVIDUAL VP. Teammates have to coordinate so they do not take each other's sector majorities.
3. Early end: the game ends when all living players belong to one team; that team wins, regardless of score. One surviving player is enough.
4. Round 30: teams compare the COMBINED VP of all their members (eliminated players count 0, as calc already does). Highest total wins. A perfect tie shows "Tie Game" with "Time for a Rematch!" underneath (no overtime yet).
5. Deficit Elimination: same walk up the score levels, except a gap is IMMUNE only if every player in the level directly ahead is a teammate of the player(s) below. Examples (x1,y1,y2,x2): 4/5/10/12 = nobody eliminated; 4/5/10/15 = only y2 eliminated (y1 is immune this round, but with y2 gone y1's next level up is x2). Elimination keeps walking past immune gaps.
6. Teammates cannot attack each other. Tapping a teammate's star as a target (own star selected) opens a popup: Connect / Gift (Cancel below).
   - Connect = a normal connection in the acting player's color, same Action cost and checks as a connection between own stars (line clear, not already linked). Raises the strength of BOTH stars.
   - Gift = the selected star is given to the teammate as if the teammate had captured it: all of its other connections are erased, ownership and color change, its Fleets go with it, one new connection to the target star is drawn at normal Action cost. Capitals cannot be gifted. The recipient can use the star on their next turn (used flags reset every turn).
7. Fleet gift (Move mode): a selected group can be given to a teammate's star in the same sector OR a star directly linked to the giving star by a Connect link. Popup: "Give N Fleets to <color>?" Yes / No. A linked target is allowed even when it lies outside the sector. No other range: an unlinked star in a different sector is not a legal target.
8. Auto-gift on elimination: when a player is eliminated, every one of their stars that is linked to a teammate's star passes to that teammate, with its Fleets. This includes a Capital. A star linked to two teammates goes to the earlier connection. Their other stars stay on the board as dead stars that anyone may capture. Applies to Deficit Elimination and Concede. (Losing the last star leaves nothing to gift; round 30 never runs deficit elimination.)
9. A survivor may capture a dead teammate's leftover stars (normal capture rules).
10. Computer players: allowed only as the SOLE member of their team (team of 1), until CPU teamwork is built. Reason: the CPU treats every other color as an enemy.
