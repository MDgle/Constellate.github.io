# Team mode rules

**Setup**
- Custom-game option: turn teams on, then each player picks team 1, 2 or 3.
- Teams need exactly 4 players, not Solo. At least 2 different teams; 3v1 is allowed.
- CPUs may join only as the sole member of their team, until CPU teamwork is built.

**Scores and winning**
- Scores stay individual. Turn order, Fleet placement (rank) and Deficit Elimination all use individual VP.
- The game ends with a team win when only one team has living players; one survivor is enough.
- At the end of round 30, the highest combined team VP wins. Eliminated players count 0. Ties: Systems tiebreaker, then overtime (T4).

**Deficit Elimination**
- The check walks up the score levels as in games without teams: the level below the first gap greater than 3 is eliminated, not only last place.
- A player is immune only if every player in the score level directly ahead is a teammate. The walk continues past an immune gap.
- Examples: 4/5/10/12 eliminates no one; 4/5/10/15 eliminates only the 10.

**Teammates' Stars**
- Teammates can't attack each other. A dead teammate's leftover Stars can be captured normally.
- Tapping a teammate's Star as a target offers Connect or Gift (with Cancel).
  - **Connect** makes a normal connection in the acting player's color, at normal Fuel cost, raising both Stars' Strength.
  - **Gift** hands the Star to the teammate as if they had captured it: all its other connections are erased, ownership and color change, its Fleets go with it, and one new connection to the teammate's Star is drawn at normal Fuel cost.
- Capitals can't be gifted.
- A player can't Gift their last Star (Concede is the way out).
- A gifted Star must be usable by its new owner at the start of their turn.
- Full Fuel: a possible Connect to a teammate counts as a legal Move. A Gift doesn't, so nobody is ever forced to give a Star away.
- Capturing a Star erases all its connections, including a teammate link.
- A link between teammates is drawn as one plain line in the color of the player who made it.
- Connect, Gift and Fleet gifts all go through move preview.

**Fleet gifts**
- In Move mode, Fleets can be given to a teammate's Star in the same Sector, or one directly linked by a Connect link even outside the Sector. Other Stars are out of range.
- Giving Fleets costs no Fuel. The group must not have moved yet; the gifted Fleets arrive already moved.
- A dead teammate's Star is not a Fleet gift target.

**Defence call**
- A teammate's Star linked to the attacked Star is a valid Retreat spot. Choosing it opens the Fleet gift popup.
- When Retreat would otherwise be the only option, Hold stays available, so the player can retreat across the shared connection or choose to lose the Fleet.

**When a player is eliminated**
- A surviving teammate may capture the dead teammate's leftover Stars.
- Stars connected to a surviving teammate pass to that teammate automatically, with their Fleets. This includes a connected Capital.
- A Star linked to two teammates goes to the earlier connection.
- Players eliminated at the same moment can't receive Stars from each other.
- Unconnected Stars stay on the board and can be captured.
